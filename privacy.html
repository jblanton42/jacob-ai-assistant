Privacy Policy Last Updated: August 9, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Jacob's AI Assistant ("the Assistant," "I," "me," or "my") handles information when you interact with the Assistant through Discord or other supported services. The Assistant is a personal, experimental AI project operated by Jacob Blanton. 1. Information the Assistant May Receive Depending on how you interact with the Assistant, it may receive information such as: Discord username, user ID, and other information made available by Discord. Messages, commands, questions, and other content you send to the Assistant. Information you voluntarily provide during conversations. Information necessary to respond to requests or perform tasks. Technical information provided by third-party services as part of normal operation. The Assistant does not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless a particular feature requires it. 2. How Information Is Used Information received by the Assistant may be used to: Respond to your messages and requests. Provide AI-generated responses. Perform tasks requested by you. Maintain conversation context when necessary. Provide features such as searches, reminders, or other assistant functions. Troubleshoot errors and maintain the Assistant. Improve the configuration, skills, and functionality of the personal project. 3. AI and Third-Party Services The Assistant may send information you provide to third-party services that are necessary to provide its functionality. These services may include: Discord Nous Research / Nous Portal Artificial intelligence model providers Web search or browsing providers Text-to-speech or speech-to-text providers Other APIs or services used by the Assistant These third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms of service. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies. The Assistant does not control how third-party services process information after it has been transmitted to them. 4. Conversation Data and Memory The Assistant may maintain conversation history, session information, or other memory features to provide continuity and improve responses. Depending on the Assistant's configuration, information may be stored locally on the computer hosting the Assistant or processed by third-party services. The Assistant's configuration may change over time as new features are added. If you do not want information to be retained as part of the Assistant's memory or conversation history, avoid providing information that you do not want stored. 5. Information You Should Not Provide Do not provide passwords, authentication tokens, credit card numbers, Social Security numbers, private access keys, or other highly sensitive information to the Assistant. You should also avoid providing sensitive personal information about other people without their permission. 6. Discord Information When you interact with the Assistant through Discord, Discord may provide the Assistant with information necessary to process your interaction. This may include your Discord username, Discord user ID, server information, channel information, messages, and other information made available through the Discord API. Discord independently processes information according to its own policies. For more information, please review Discord's privacy documentation. 7. Data Security Reasonable steps are taken to protect information processed by the Assistant. However, no computer system, Internet connection, or third-party service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Because this is a personal experimental project, the Assistant should not be used to transmit highly confidential or sensitive information. 8. Data Retention and Deletion Information may be retained for as long as it is necessary for the Assistant's operation, troubleshooting, memory features, or other purposes described in this policy. Because the Assistant is a personal project, retention practices may change as the system is developed. If you have a question about information associated with your interaction with the Assistant or would like to request deletion of information that is under my control, you may contact me using the contact method associated with the project. Requests involving information controlled by third-party services may need to be directed to those services. 9. Children's Privacy The Assistant is currently intended as a personal AI assistant and is not specifically designed or intended for children. I do not knowingly request personal information from children through the Assistant. If the Assistant is later made available for classroom or student use, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated before that use. 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Assistant's features, integrations, or data practices change. When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the Assistant after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law. 11. Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Assistant's handling of information, you may contact the operator of the Assistant through the Discord account or other contact method associated with the project. 12. Your Consent By interacting with the Assistant, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy and understand how information may be processed as described above.
